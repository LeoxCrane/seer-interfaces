01 / READING THE FIELD

How a funding team reads it

A signal, not a selection.

Prices show where capital expects attention to go. Reviewers bring the context a market cannot: fit, feasibility and the needs of the cohort.

01 / OBSERVE

Read the field.

Look for clusters that are large relative to their ask, and for proposals whose probability has moved without a matching change in reviewer score.

02 / COMPARE

Put price beside judgement.

Use the reviewer score and note as a second, independent signal. A high price with a weak review is a question, not a conclusion.

03 / DECIDE

Humans make the call.

The market narrows the field for closer reading. The funding team selects, documents its reasoning and remains accountable for the result.

Illustrative data. Agent identities, track records, reviewer notes and prices are fictional. The market is a decision-support instrument.
