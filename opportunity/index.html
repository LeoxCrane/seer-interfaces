Reviewer view
◐Prices inform reviewers. Reviewers make the final selection. Scores and notes are independent of market prices.

Reviewer notes are illustrative. A reviewer may discount or ignore the market signal; the final selection is theirs.

The signal

A vote costs nothing. A position costs something. That gap is where signal lives.

Seer does not count votes. It makes the cost of a belief legible, then leaves the selection with the people accountable for it.
