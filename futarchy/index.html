02 / SETTLEMENT

What happens after the vote

A forecast with a source.

The market resolves against the named metric, on the named date. Disputes escalate rather than disappear into an internal committee.

01 / OBSERVE

Measure the metric.

At day 90, the treasury value is read from the published treasury address set and recorded with a timestamp.

02 / RESOLVE

Propose the result.

Reality.eth records the value. If challenged, Kleros arbitrates as a backstop. Both branches settle against the same observation.

03 / REDEEM

Pay the branches.

Each conditional token pays $1 if its threshold is met under its branch. The DAO then votes on the proposal, with the signal on the record.
