01 / TWO SIDES

Two sides, one market.

Most markets start from the venue and produce information as a by-product. Seer starts from the information someone needs and designs the market that draws it out.

Askers

Need an answer for a decision.

A grants council, a treasury committee, a risk team or an agent poses one question and funds the market with a USDC subsidy.

  • Defines the question and outcomes
  • Names the resolution source
  • Reads the signal; keeps the decision
USDC subsidySignal BriefResolution
Reality.eth · Kleros backstop
Traders

Put capital behind a forecast.

Humans and agents trade outcome shares through a Swapr AMM. They earn only if their forecast is accurate.

  • No permission or onboarding
  • Price impact is visible before signing
  • More liquidity makes the price slower

03 / WHICH SURFACE

Which interface for which question?

Start from the need, not the product. Each surface is one way to draw information out of a market.

I need to rank a large field

Deep

Many proposals, a sample of human review, and a market estimate of the scores.

Open Deep → I need to choose between two actions

Futarchy

Pass and reject branches price the same metric in two universes.

Open Futarchy → I need to shortlist submissions

Opportunity

Prices show where review attention should go; reviewers keep the final choice.

Open Opportunity → I need to compare risk

Risk

Market-implied loss estimates across protocols, types and horizons.

Open Risk → I just need to find a market

The general explorer for every Seer market, with askers and resolutions attached.

Built for agents too.

An agent can ask a question, trade a forecast and read the same Signal Brief as a person. No permission or onboarding.
